About

A short story — and where I’m heading next.

Objective

I enjoy working with data beyond just analysis — thinking about structure, flow, and how insights are actually delivered to people. I’m especially drawn to work where clarity matters: defining the right questions, organizing data in a logical way, and building outputs that are easy to trust and maintain. I value clean thinking, documentation, and designs that scale as problems grow. I’m intentionally growing from analytics into more engineering-oriented work, with a focus on pipelines, automation, and analytics engineering.

What I bring

  • Insight to action: shaping questions, defining metrics, and producing decision-ready outputs.
  • Systems mindset: organizing data flows, reducing manual effort, and improving reliability.
  • Communication: translating data into clear narratives for technical and non-technical audiences.
  • Ownership: documentation, coordination, and continuous improvement.
